About Mailzy

A small, independent project — not a company.

What this is

Mailzy gives you a real, working email address the instant you load the page — no signup, no password. It's built for the moment you need an inbox to catch one email (a signup confirmation, a one-time verification code) without handing over your real address.

How it actually works

Mailzy is entirely client-side — there's no server or database behind it. When you load the page, your browser talks directly to mail.gw, a free third-party mail API, to create a mailbox and poll it for new messages. We don't see, store, or have access to anything sent to your temporary address; it goes straight from mail.gw to your browser.

Who's behind it

Mailzy is an independent, open-source side project. The full source code is public on GitHub, where you can read exactly how it works, report a bug, or suggest a change.

What it isn't

It isn't a private mailbox, and it isn't meant for anything long-term or sensitive. Anyone who has (or guesses) your temporary address can read what's sent to it. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail on what is and isn't collected.
